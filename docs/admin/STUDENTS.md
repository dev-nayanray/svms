# Students

The Students module is the heart of Euroscope. Every student has a **Student 360** page that shows their entire journey in one place.

## What You Can Do Here

- View all students in a searchable, filterable table
- Add new students
- View a student's complete profile (Student 360)
- Edit student information
- Archive students (soft delete — records are preserved)
- View student's applications, documents, payments, tasks, and timeline

## How to Add a Student

1. Go to **CRM → Students**
2. Click **Add Student**
3. Fill in the required fields:
   - First name and last name
   - Email address (must be unique)
   - Phone number
   - Date of birth
   - Nationality
4. Add optional information:
   - Academic records
   - English proficiency scores
   - Passport details
   - Emergency contact
5. Click **Save**

## Student 360 Page

Click any student to see their complete profile:

- **Header** — name, student ID, status, photo
- **Personal Information** — contact details, date of birth, nationality
- **Academic Records** — education history
- **English Proficiency** — IELTS/TOEFL scores
- **Passport Information** — passport number, expiry (masked for security)
- **Applications** — all applications for this student
- **Documents** — all documents uploaded by/for this student
- **Payments & Invoices** — financial history
- **Tasks** — assigned tasks
- **Timeline** — chronological activity log

## Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Active | Currently being processed |
| Inactive | Not currently active (paused, graduated, etc.) |
| Archived | Soft-deleted — hidden from lists but records preserved |

## Search & Filter

- **Search** by name, email, or student ID
- **Filter** by status (Active, Inactive, Archived)
- **Sort** by name, created date, or status
- **Pagination** — 20 students per page
